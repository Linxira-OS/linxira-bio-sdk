## What you already have, at no cost / 你已经无偿拥有的

The AGPL already covers:

- running the project locally, on your own machines, inside your organisation, for
  internal work — including commercial and revenue-generating work;
- academic, teaching and personal use;
- modifying it, and redistributing your version under the AGPL;
- offering it to users over a network, provided those users receive the corresponding
  source of your modified version, as AGPL §13 requires.

Using this project at work is therefore **not** a commercial-licensing question, and
you do not need to contact anyone. 在公司内使用本项目属于 AGPL 已授权范围，不需要联系
维护者，也不需要购买任何东西。

## When a commercial license is the right answer / 什么时候商业授权是对的答案

- You want to embed the code in a closed-source product, or run a hosted service, and
  your legal team will not accept AGPL §13's source-provision duty.
- Your organisation's policy bans AGPL/GPL-family software outright, whatever the
  technical facts.
- You need something the AGPL does not give: an indemnity, a warranty, a support or
  uptime commitment, or the right to keep your modifications private.

In those cases the same code can be licensed under separate negotiated terms. That is
what "dual licensing" means here: one code base, two paperwork paths, and no second-class
build.

## What we will not do / 我们不会做的

- **No license keys, activation, telemetry or feature gating.** The released build is
  the complete product; the AGPL option stays available to everyone.
- **No "open core".** Nothing is held back for the commercial tier, and no capability is
  crippled to create a reason to buy. Same code, different paperwork.
- **No relicensing of other people's work.** A contribution can be offered commercially
  only when its author granted that right (see `CONTRIBUTING.md`); the maintainers do
  not relicense anyone's code behind their back.

## How to start / 怎么开始

Open a public issue in this repository titled `commercial license`, describing the
deployment shape you have in mind. Please keep confidential terms out of that issue —
it is only the entry point, and negotiation then moves to a private channel.

> Maintainer contact for licensing: **to be published here before the first agreement is
> signed** — today the issue above is the entry point. 维护者的授权联系渠道：在签署首份
> 协议之前会公布于此；目前入口是上面那个 issue。

Terms and pricing are negotiated per deployment; nothing on this page commits the
maintainers to a price, a scope or a delivery date. 授权条款与价格逐案商议，本页不构成
任何报价或承诺。

## Not legal advice / 不是法律意见

This page is a statement of intent by the maintainers, not legal advice, and it is not
part of any contract until a separate written agreement says so. If your legal team
needs a different arrangement than the two paths above, ask — the worst case is that we
say no, in writing, which is more useful than guessing.
