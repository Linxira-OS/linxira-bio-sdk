## 1. Values（我们认可的行为标准）

This project is built and maintained by people who believe that technical
work happens between human beings, and that the following conduct is a
precondition for cooperation of any kind — employment, contribution,
adoption, or partnership:

- **No psychological manipulation in professional settings.** Interviews,
  code reviews, negotiations, and community interactions must be free of
  PUA-style practices: systematic belittling of candidates or contributors,
  fabricated negative feedback, deliberately manufactured stress to
  suppress compensation or credit, or exploitation of information asymmetry
  (e.g. take-home work extracted under interview pretenses with no
  intention to hire).
- **Honest dealing.** Claims about one's own products, benchmarks, or
  intentions should survive scrutiny. We hold ourselves to the same
  standard — our benchmark methodology and its honest caveats are published
  in `benchmark-results/`.
- **Respect for labor.** We do not endorse "more hours" as a proxy for
  "more value". Sustainable pace is an engineering requirement, not a
  luxury.

本项目由相信"技术协作以人的尊严为前提"的人维护。任何形式的合作——雇佣、
贡献、采用、商业合伙——都以下述行为标准为前提：**专业场景中禁止精神操纵
（PUA）**（系统性贬低候选人/贡献者、虚构负面评价、以制造压力压制薪酬或
署名、以面试为名榨取方案而无招聘意图等）；**诚实交易**（自家产品与基准的
宣称要经得起检验，我们对自己的基准同样公开全部口径与反面注记）；**尊重
劳动**（不把"工时更长"当"价值更高"，可持续节奏是工程要求而非奢侈品）。

## 2. Maintainer position（维护者立场）

The maintainer has personally experienced — in offline technical interviews
with companies in the bioinformatics industry — conduct of the kind
described in §1, and knows of other candidates with similar experiences.
Because the incidents were not recorded at the time, **no specific company
is named in this file today**; naming an entity without a verifiable public
record would expose the maintainer to defamation risk and this project to
needless conflict.

据此，维护者声明：

1. The maintainer **declines collaboration, advisory support, co-marketing,
   and endorsement** with entities whose conduct in technical interviews or
   business dealings materially violates §1. This is an expression of the
   maintainer's own position; it is not a license term and it does not
   restrict anyone's use of the code.
2. The maintainer keeps a **private, dated record** of such incidents. If a
   pattern is ever corroborated by verifiable public records (court filings,
   regulatory decisions, multiple independent public accounts), the
   maintainer reserves the right to publish a fact-based entry in a
   `HALL_OF_SHAME.md`, FFmpeg-style, restricted to **documented license
   violations** and **publicly corroborated conduct** — behavior-focused,
   never person-focused.
3. Entity identifiers (including unified social credit codes) would appear
   only at that stage, and only attached to the underlying records.

维护者在生物信息行业的线下技术面试中亲身经历过 §1 所述行为，并知悉其他
候选人有类似经历。**因当时未留存可公开核验的记录，本文件今日不点名任何
具体公司**——无实证的点名会让维护者暴露于名誉权风险，也让项目卷入无谓冲
突。维护者保留私下带日期的记录；若未来出现可公开核验的模式（司法文书、
监管认定、多个独立公开来源），维护者保留以 FFmpeg 式 `HALL_OF_SHAME.md`
**基于事实**公开的权利——只记录可查证的许可证违规与公开佐证的行为，对事
不对人；实体标识（含统一社会信用代码）仅在该阶段、且附着于底层记录时出现。

## 3. What this file is NOT（本文件不是什么）

- It is **not** a license clause. The AGPL-3.0-or-later license is
  unchanged; no usage restriction exists or may be inferred from this file.
- It is **not** a claim about any specific company. No entity is named
  above.
- It does **not** affect the project's open-source status. OSI's Open
  Source Definition bars discriminatory *license* terms; a non-binding
  values statement outside the license is the community-accepted way to
  express a position (cf. FFmpeg's Hall of Shame — a separate page, not a
  license term; the Anti-996 License — rejected by OSI precisely because it
  put such terms *inside* a license).

本文件不是许可证条款；不对任何具体公司作出指控；不影响项目开源属性。
OSI 开源定义禁止的是**许可证内**的歧视性条款；在许可证之外以非约束性文件
表达立场，是社区已验证的路径（FFmpeg 耻辱榜即独立页面而非许可证条款；反
996 许可证被 OSI 拒绝，恰因把条款放进了许可证内部）。

## 4. Trademark and endorsement（商标与背书）

The project name, logo, and the "Linxira" marks may **not** be used by any
entity in a way that implies endorsement, partnership, or certification by
the project — regardless of this statement — except with written permission.
Referencing the project descriptively ("built on linxira-bio-sdk, AGPL-3.0")
is always fine and encouraged.

任何实体不得以暗示获得本项目背书、合作或认证的方式使用项目名称/Logo/"Linxira"
标识（无论本声明如何）。描述性引用（"基于 linxira-bio-sdk，AGPL-3.0"）永远
欢迎。

## 5. Feedback channel（回应通道）

An entity that believes it has been affected by this statement, or wishes to
respond to a future published record, may open a private channel with the
maintainers; published records will link or quote the response verbatim.

认为受本声明影响的实体，或希望回应未来公开记录的实体，可与维护者建立私下
沟通渠道；已公开的记录将原文链接或引用其回应。
